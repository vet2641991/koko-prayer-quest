Privacy Policy — Koko

By DHM Institute · Last updated: 23 October 2026

Koko is a learning app for children (Arabic, the prayer, Islamic Studies and short surahs of the Qur'an) made by DHM Institute. This policy covers the website koko.dhm-institute.com and the Koko app for Android and iOS ("Koko: Arabic & Quran for Kids" in Google Play and the App Store). We built it so children can learn safely. In short: we collect no personal information about your child. If a parent chooses Koko Premium (a paid subscription), we keep the parent's email address and the subscription's status, and nothing else.

What stays on the device

The child's first name (used only to greet them in the app), stars, lesson progress and learning time are saved only on the child's own device (browser storage on the website, app storage in the phone app). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the browser or app data, or deleting the app, deletes them.

Lessons downloaded to the device

Lessons, sounds, pictures and videos are downloaded from our website and kept on the device so the app also works offline. These downloads carry no personal information. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security; we do not use them to identify anyone.

Microphone

Some lessons let the child record their voice and play it back to compare with Koko. The recording stays on the device only while the child is on that screen: it is never uploaded or shared, and it is deleted when the child leaves the screen. The microphone is used only after the device asks for permission.

Koko Premium and the parent account (optional)

Koko Premium is a monthly subscription for parents that opens every lesson in the app. It is bought by a grown-up, behind the parental gate, in the App Store, in Google Play, or on our website. Children are never asked for an email or a payment.

  • Parent's email address: only if a parent creates a family account (to use Koko Premium on several devices or with the website) or subscribes on the website. It is used to sign in with a one-time code and to link the subscription to the family. We do not send marketing emails from it.
  • Subscription status: whether Koko Premium is active, until when, whether it renews, where it was bought (App Store, Google Play or website) and the product. No card numbers: we never see or store payment details.
  • Payments are handled by Apple, Google or Stripe under their own privacy policies. For website subscriptions we keep Stripe's customer number, to open Stripe's page where the parent manages or cancels.

Our service providers, who process this data only for us: Supabase (our database and sign-in emails, hosted in the European Union), RevenueCat (checks App Store and Google Play purchases; it receives the purchase receipt, an account number and basic device information such as the app version and country), Stripe (website payments) and our email delivery provider (sign-in codes). None of them may use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting. The parent's email and subscription status are kept while the account exists. A parent can delete the account at any time in the app (Parents → Family account → Delete account) or by asking us; it is deleted at once, and a website subscription is cancelled with it. Subscriptions bought in the App Store or Google Play are cancelled in the store's settings. Payment records that the law requires are kept by Apple, Google or Stripe.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking or analytics, no location, no contacts, no sharing or selling of data, no chat with strangers, and no account or personal information for children.

Links to our website

The app has buttons and short messages for parents, such as "Book a free trial class", that open our website, dhm-institute.com, in the browser. In the phone app, a grown-up must first answer a question a young child cannot (a parental gate). Anything entered on the website is covered by the website's own policy, not by the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact DHM Institute through dhm-institute.com.
