Privacy Policy — Koko

By DHM Institute · Last updated: 22 October 2026

Koko is a learning app for children (Arabic, the prayer, Islamic Studies and short surahs of the Qur'an) made by DHM Institute. This policy covers the website koko.dhm-institute.com and the Koko app for Android and iOS ("Koko: Arabic & Quran for Kids" in Google Play and the App Store). We built it so children can learn safely. In short: we do not collect any personal information.

What stays on the device

The child's first name (used only to greet them in the app), stars, lesson progress and learning time are saved only on the child's own device (browser storage on the website, app storage in the phone app). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the browser or app data, or deleting the app, deletes them.

Lessons downloaded to the device

Lessons, sounds, pictures and videos are downloaded from our website and kept on the device so the app also works offline. These downloads carry no personal information. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security; we do not use them to identify anyone.

Microphone

Some lessons let the child record their voice and play it back to compare with Koko. The recording stays on the device only while the child is on that screen: it is never uploaded or shared, and it is deleted when the child leaves the screen. The microphone is used only after the device asks for permission.

What we do not do

No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking or analytics, no location, no contacts, no sharing or selling of data, and no chat with strangers.

Links to our website

The app has buttons and short messages for parents, such as "Book a free trial class", that open our website, dhm-institute.com, in the browser. In the phone app, a grown-up must first answer a question a young child cannot (a parental gate). Anything entered on the website is covered by the website's own policy, not by the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact DHM Institute through dhm-institute.com.
