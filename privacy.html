Privacy Policy — Koko's Quest

By DHM Institute · Last updated: 12 October 2026

Koko's Quest is a learning app for children (Arabic, the prayer and Islamic Studies) made by DHM Institute. We built it so children can learn safely. In short: we do not collect any personal information.

What stays on the device

The child's first name (used only to greet them in the app), stars, lesson progress and learning time are saved only on the child's own device (browser storage). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the browser or app data deletes them.

Microphone

Some lessons let the child record their voice and play it back to compare with Koko. The recording stays in the device's memory, is never uploaded or saved, and disappears when the lesson screen is closed. The microphone is used only after the device asks for permission.

What we do not do

No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking or analytics, no location, no contacts, no sharing or selling of data, and no chat with strangers.

Links to our website

The app has a "Book a free trial class" button that opens our website, dhm-institute.com, in the browser. It is meant for parents. Anything entered on the website is covered by the website's own policy, not by the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact DHM Institute through dhm-institute.com.
